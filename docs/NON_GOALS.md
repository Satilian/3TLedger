# Non-Goals

3T Ledger intentionally does NOT aim to become:

- A banking application.
- A cryptocurrency exchange.
- A stock trading platform.
- A payment processor.
- A tax preparation service.
- An ERP replacement.
- A CRM.
- A note-taking application.
- A messenger.

These integrations may exist.

But they are not the mission.

---

Our mission is simple:

Increase financial transparency.

Increase trust.

Preserve history.
