# Philosophy

> **Software should not only manage money.**
>
> **It should increase trust between people.**

---

# The Purpose

Money is not the problem.

Lack of clarity is.

Financial disagreements often begin with questions like:

- "Who paid?"
- "Where did the money go?"
- "Was this approved?"
- "Why are the numbers different?"

3T Ledger exists to replace assumptions with facts.

The goal is not accounting.

The goal is trust.

---

# Transparency

Transparency is not exposing everything.

Transparency means that everyone can clearly understand the information they are entitled to see.

Good transparency answers questions before they become arguments.

---

# Trust

Trust should emerge naturally from the system.

People should not have to remember.

People should not need screenshots.

People should not need spreadsheets.

People should not need to ask.

The system should provide the answer.

---

# Trail

History matters.

Every meaningful action should leave a permanent trace.

Not because we expect mistakes.

But because understanding the past makes better decisions possible.

History is a feature.

Not a log.

---

# Ownership

Users own their information.

Organizations own their infrastructure.

Developers own their extensions.

3T Ledger owns nothing that belongs to its users.

---

# Simplicity

Complexity is expensive.

Every new feature increases the cost of understanding.

Every new dependency increases the cost of maintenance.

Every abstraction must justify its existence.

Simple systems survive longer.

---

# Technology

Technology is never the objective.

React is replaceable.

NestJS is replaceable.

PostgreSQL is replaceable.

Cloud providers are replaceable.

The principles are not.

---

# Scale

The same ideas should work for:

- one person;
- one family;
- one company;
- one community;
- one government;
- the entire world.

Only the scale changes.

The philosophy does not.

---

# Open

The project should remain open in every possible sense:

- open architecture;
- open standards;
- open APIs;
- open documentation;
- open discussion.

Openness creates innovation.

---

# Longevity

Build software that can still be maintained in twenty years.

Avoid trends.

Prefer fundamentals.

Good ideas age slowly.

---

# Decision Rule

Before implementing any feature, ask:

Does it improve:

- Transparency?
- Trust?
- Trail?

If it improves none of them,

it probably does not belong in 3T Ledger.

---

# Final Thought

> **Transparency creates Trust.**
>
> **Trust is preserved by Trail.**
>
> **That is the purpose of 3T Ledger.**
