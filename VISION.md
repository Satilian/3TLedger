# Vision

Imagine a world where financial disagreements are rare.

Not because people are perfect.

But because information is clear.

A family never argues about who paid.

A startup always knows where its runway goes.

A charity can prove where every donation was spent.

A city can explain every public expense.

A government can earn trust through transparency.

The technology is the easy part.

Building trust is the real mission.

That is why 3T Ledger exists.
